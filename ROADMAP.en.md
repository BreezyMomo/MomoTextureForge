# Texture tool features

<!-- doc-language -->
[简体中文](ROADMAP.md) · [English](ROADMAP.en.md)
<!-- /doc-language -->

The five implementation stages and acceptance criteria are in the [expansion plan](plans/texture-tool-expansion/README.en.md), executed in numerical order.

The 3D material preview supports sphere, plane and cube, with lighting, roughness, metalness and texture-repeat controls. Normal generation supports Sobel/Scharr, flips and pre-smoothing. Materials are sampled locally in the browser and work in static builds.

Potential future work includes AO, normals from four directional photographs, custom models and more output channels, according to actual demand.
